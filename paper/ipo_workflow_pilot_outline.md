# Paper outline — Can Benchmark Skills Compose into an IPO Workflow?

## Working title

**Can Benchmark Skills Compose into an IPO Workflow? Evaluating Finance Agents Across Departmental Handoffs**

Potential result-driven social title:

**Every Agent Passed Its Benchmark. The Bank Still Failed.**

## Abstract claim boundary

The pilot does not claim empirical performance for frontier models. It validates an environment and scoring design that separates department-level correctness from enterprise-level coordination. The paper-scale study will test whether isolated finance benchmark scores predict end-to-end IPO workflow success.

## Research questions

1. Do isolated finance benchmark scores predict multi-department workflow success?
2. Does a specialized multi-agent team outperform one generalist agent after handoff errors are counted?
3. Do model and harness rankings remain stable as evaluation moves from isolated tasks to an enterprise workflow?
4. How much performance is lost to stale evidence, scenario mismatch, provenance loss, and cross-artifact inconsistency?

## Planned conditions

- isolated department tasks;
- one generalist agent;
- four specialized departmental agents;
- specialized agents plus a coordinator/verifier;
- initial S-1 only;
- S-1 followed by an S-1/A amendment.

## Primary outcomes

- component score;
- coordination score;
- workflow score;
- composition gap;
- enterprise success;
- ranking correlation and pairwise rank reversals;
- cost and latency per successful workflow.

## Minimum paper-scale experiment

- 4 public IPO families;
- 3–4 frontier models, including at least two Chinese-lab models if APIs are accessible;
- 3 orchestration conditions;
- 2 evidence rounds;
- 2 repeated runs for a stability subset;
- public deterministic verifiers and trajectory logs.

## Contribution target

The contribution is not a generic simulated bank. It is a paired compositional-validity study measuring whether skills represented by existing finance benchmarks remain predictive when joined through departmental handoffs in one persistent IPO workflow.
